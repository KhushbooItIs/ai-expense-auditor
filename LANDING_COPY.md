## FAQ

**Q: Is this actually free?**
A: Yes. No credit card, no login, no catch. We make money by connecting teams with large savings to Credex — a service that sources discounted AI credits. If we don't find you savings, we don't surface Credex. That's the alignment.

**Q: How do you know what I should be paying?**
A: We pull pricing from official vendor pages weekly and store it in a database. Every recommendation traces back to a public URL. A finance person can verify every number. We never invent figures — if we can't confirm a price, we don't make a recommendation.

**Q: Who sees my data?**
A: Nobody. Your tool spend and team size are used to compute the audit and never shared. If you submit your email for the PDF report, that's stored securely and only used to send you the report. The public share link never includes your email or company name.

**Q: What tools do you cover?**
A: Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Claude, ChatGPT, Anthropic API, OpenAI API, Gemini, and Windsurf. We add tools based on what users request — if yours isn't listed, email us.

**Q: What's Credex?**
A: Credex sources discounted AI infrastructure credits from companies that overforecast their usage or pivoted. If your audit shows significant savings, Credex can often beat those savings further through bulk credits. We only surface them for high-savings audits — we don't push it if it's not relevant to you.
