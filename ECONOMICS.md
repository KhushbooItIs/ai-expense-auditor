# Unit Economics

## What a converted lead is worth to Credex

Credex sources discounted AI infrastructure credits. A startup buying through Credex instead of retail typically saves 20–40% on tools like Cursor, Claude, and ChatGPT Enterprise.

**Assumptions (conservative):**
- Average startup AI spend: $800/mo ($9,600/yr)
- Credex discount sourced: 25% off retail
- Credex margin on the discount: 10% of retail price
- Average contract length: 12 months

**LTV per converted customer:**
```
Startup annual spend:       $9,600
Credex margin (10%):          $960
Average contract length:    1.5 yr  (some churn, some expand)
LTV:                        ~$1,440
```

This is conservative. High-savings audits (>$500/mo detected waste) skew toward larger teams with $2,000–5,000/mo spend. For those:

```
Annual spend:              $36,000
Credex margin (10%):        $3,600
LTV at 1.5 yr:             ~$5,400
```

## Conversion funnel

```
Visitors to form                100%
Audit completed                  60%   (drop-off: confused by form, wrong audience)
Email captured                    8%   (of completions = ~5% of visitors)
Credex consult booked             2%   (of completions = high-savings route only)
Credit purchase                  50%   (of consults booked)
```

**What makes this profitable:**

At 1,000 audits/month:
- 600 completed
- 48 email captures (leads)
- 12 consult bookings (high-savings route)
- 6 purchases at avg $1,440 LTV = **$8,640 gross value/month from 1,000 visitors**

Tool cost at 1,000 audits: ~$0.10 (OpenAI) + ~$0 (Resend) + ~$7 (Render) = **< $10/month**

The unit economics work from day one if Credex closes even 3–4 deals per month.

## Path to $1M ARR in 18 months

**What has to be true:**

| Milestone | When | Requires |
|---|---|---|
| 100 audits/day | Month 2 | HN launch + 1 viral share |
| 500 audits/day | Month 5 | Ongoing Reddit/X + word of mouth from saved users |
| 30 consults/month | Month 4 | 1,500 audits/month, 2% consult rate |
| 15 deals/month at $960 avg | Month 6 | 50% close rate on consults |
| $14,400 MRR | Month 6 | 15 deals × $960 avg annual ÷ 12 |
| $83,000 MRR | Month 18 | Scale to 150 deals/month |

**$1M ARR = $83,333 MRR**

To hit this in 18 months requires ~150 new Credex customers/month by month 18. At a 2% audit → consult rate, that means **7,500 audits/month** by month 18 (~250/day).

250 audits/day is plausible if:
1. The tool goes viral once (HN front page = 500 audits in 48 hrs)
2. SEO kicks in on "AI tool pricing comparison" queries (3–6 month lag)
3. Credex sales team uses the tool as a prospecting qualifier

**The key assumption to stress-test:** 2% consult-to-purchase rate. If it's 1%, the path requires 2x the audit volume. If it's 5% (warmer leads from Credex list), the path is 2x faster.
